# Format: short from a long-form video

Read by `repurpose`. A good short cut from a long video is a complete video on its own, not a trailer for the long one.

## What makes a moment worth cutting

Look for a stretch of 20-60 seconds that has:
- **One complete point**: a tip, a result, a strong opinion, a surprising fact, a story with a punchline, a before/after or a demo with a visible result.
- **A hookable start**: a line, or a reshuffled line from later, that would stop the scroll on its own.
- **A payoff** inside the clip.
- **No dependence** on what came before ("as I said earlier", "this tool" with no name).

Good sources: the long video's best retention moments (spikes in YouTube Analytics' retention graph, when available), the comments people quote, the payoff of each section, strong opinions, and demos with a visible "wow".

## Two ways to make it

1. **Clip:** cut the original footage. Fastest. Needs a hook: either a strong first line already in the clip, or a new hook recorded or added as on-screen text, or a line moved from later in the clip to the front.
2. **Rewrite:** a fresh short-form script on the same point, recorded new. Better when the long-form delivery is slow or depends on context. Use the short-form rules (hook, open loop, value, payoff).

## Template (per short)

- **Working name**
- **Source:** long-form idea and timestamps (start-end)
- **Method:** clip or rewrite
- **The one point**
- **Hook:** spoken line + on-screen text + opening visual. For a clip, where the hook comes from (timestamp) or what's added
- **Cut plan (clip):** in/out points, any sections removed, what moves to the front
- **Script (rewrite):** the short-form script
- **Payoff line**
- **On-screen text and captions:** word-by-word captions, highlight keywords
- **CTA:** usually none, or "full video on my channel" only if it genuinely adds; a DM keyword if the offer uses one
- **Caption:** first line restates the promise; then context; keyword CTA if any
- **Length:** 20-60s

## Rules

- Each short makes one point. Three tips from one section are three shorts.
- Never start with context or a greeting. Start on the hook.
- Remove filler, pauses and repeated sentences. Shorts run faster than long form.
- Vertical 9:16, face or subject centred, captions in the middle third (clear of the platform UI at top and bottom).
- Aim for 3-8 shorts from a 10-20 minute video. Rank them by how strong the hook and payoff are.
